Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Common Grounds is a friendship-first service for meeting people nearby through shared interests. This policy explains the information we use and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide to create and use your account, including authentication information from Google or Apple, your profile details (such as name, photo, bio, and interests), messages, and reports or blocks you submit. We also process device location when you choose to enable discoverability, and may store a device notification token so we can deliver service notifications.

How location works

Location is optional. It is used to find compatible members nearby while you are discoverable. Other members see only a broad distance band. Common Grounds does not show another member your exact coordinates or venue. Discoverability expires shortly after the app leaves the foreground, and you can turn it off at any time.

How we use information

We use information to provide matching, messaging, safety features, account support, and service security. We do not sell your personal information or use your location to serve third-party advertising.

Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to operate the service, including with Firebase and Google Cloud services that provide authentication, data storage, notifications, and backend processing. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect people, our service, or our rights.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app’s Settings. Deletion permanently removes your profile, profile photo, waves, connections, conversations, messages, reports associated with your account, and Firebase Authentication record from active systems. Limited information may remain in backups or be retained where required for security or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can update your profile, control discoverability, block members, report safety concerns, and delete your account in the app. You may also contact us to request help with your information.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email commonask3@gmail.com.
