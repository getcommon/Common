Community Guidelines

Effective September 27, 2026

Common Grounds exists for considerate, low-pressure friendship. Help make it safe, welcoming, and real.

Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, threaten, shame, or target someone based on identity, background, appearance, beliefs, disability, or any protected characteristic.

Keep it consensual and appropriate

Do not send unwanted sexual content, solicit sexual services, pressure someone for a response, or share intimate material. Respect a no, a block, an unmatch, or silence.

Protect privacy and safety

Do not share another person’s personal information, precise location, private messages, or images without permission. Never use Common Grounds to scam, deceive, stalk, exploit, or facilitate illegal activity.

Be genuine

Do not impersonate others, create deceptive accounts, manipulate people, spam, or use the service for unsolicited promotion.

Report concerns

Use the in-app Report and Block controls when something feels unsafe or violates these guidelines. For support, email commonask3@gmail.com. We may remove content, limit access, or remove accounts to protect the community.
